How a deal gets onto the channel

  1. We find an offer

    From store offer feeds, deal listings, and products we look up ourselves.

  2. We check the price

    We compare it with the prices we have recorded for that item. A price that never changes, next to an inflated MRP, is not a deal.

  3. A person approves it

    Nothing is posted automatically. Every post is reviewed by a person first.

  4. We post it and keep it current

    Each post shows the price at the time of posting. When an offer ends, we edit the post to say so.

What we won’t do

  • We don’t invent countdowns. A deadline appears only when the store gave us one.
  • We don’t say “only a few left” unless the store reports it.
  • We don’t call a price the lowest without recorded prices to back it up.
  • We don’t post between midnight and 7 am IST.

How we earn

Links in our posts go to Amazon.in, Flipkart and other stores through affiliate programmes. If you buy after clicking one, we may earn a commission. It costs you nothing extra and does not change the price you pay.
